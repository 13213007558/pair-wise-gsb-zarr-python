Added streaming reductions to arrays: `Array.sum`, `Array.mean`, and
`Array.variance` (plus their `AsyncArray` counterparts) compute statistics
chunk by chunk, so the working set scales with the chunk size rather than the
total number of elements. The reductions support reducing over one or more
axes, `keepdims`, writing into a caller-provided `out` array, a configurable
accumulator `dtype`, `ddof` for variance, and a `nan_policy` of
`"propagate"` or `"omit"`. Per-chunk statistics are merged with the
numerically stable parallel algorithm of Chan et al., and unwritten chunks
contribute the array's `fill_value` as observations.
